    Where models come from

      Any OpenAI-compatible service works. Keys are kept in a file on this machine (data/keys), never in the config or the published copy.

      Change who plays

      The new model plays from this house's next turn and inherits its land, notebook and agreements. It is told it took over, and the other houses are told the house is now played by someone new.
